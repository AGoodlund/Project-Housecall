Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about our website. If you still need help, click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner.

What is HouseCall?

HouseCall helps users find healthcare providers and view their contact details, locations, and appointment information automatically using AI.

How does the AI find healthcare provider information?

Our AI scans provider websites and extracts key data like phone numbers, locations, and appointment dates automatically from official health provider websites.

Do I need to create an account to use this website?

No, you can search for providers without creating an account. The service is completely free to use.

Do you collect my personal information?

No, we don't collect or store personal user data. We only process publicly available provider information to help you find healthcare services.

How accurate is the information?

The data comes directly from provider websites, and our AI checks for the most recent updates to keep results accurate.

Can I contact providers directly?

Yes, each provider's page includes their official contact information and links for appointments. You can use the phone numbers or links shown in the results to reach them directly.

What if I find incorrect information?

You can use our feedback form to report incorrect details. Click here to access the feedback form.

Who created this website?

It was developed by a student team for an AI and innovation hackathon to make healthcare information easier to access.

I still need help. What should I do?

If you still have questions, click the chat icon in the bottom-right corner to open our chatbox and get support.
